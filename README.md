## Privacy Policy

### 1. Scope

This policy explains how wang yupeng handles information in version 1.0 of Sohbi. Sohbi is a local personal journal with optional on-device emotion analysis.

### 2. Information Stored in the App

Your journal text, mood tags, selected photos, dates, and available emotion analysis results are stored locally on your device. Settings such as your selected language and reminder time are also stored locally.

We do not receive this journal content, upload it to our servers, sell it, or share it with advertisers. Version 1.0 has no account system, cloud synchronization, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

### 3. On-Device Emotion Analysis

When Apple Intelligence is available on a compatible iPhone running iOS 26 or later, Sohbi uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to analyze entry text. Sohbi does not send that text to a cloud AI service for analysis. Results are saved locally with the entry.

The feature depends on Apple Intelligence device, language, region, and model availability. Journaling and manual mood tagging remain available without analysis. Apple's handling of operating-system services and model downloads is governed by Apple's own policies.

### 4. Optional Permissions

- **Camera:** Used when you choose to take a photo for an entry.
- **Photos:** The system photo picker lets you choose images to import. Imported copies are stored with the entry.
- **Notifications:** Used only for an optional daily reminder scheduled on your device. The reminder does not include your journal text.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Text journaling does not require these permissions.

### 5. Retention, Deletion, and Backups

Entries remain in the app until you delete them or the app's local data is removed. You can delete an entry from its detail screen. Deleting an entry does not delete an original photo from your photo library.

Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, generally removes its local app data. Device backups may retain copies according to your iOS or computer backup settings. Deleting app data does not necessarily delete those backups. We do not manage or have access to your device backups and cannot restore lost entries for you.
